Digitizing Trade and Regulatory Operations

Overview

Pakistan Single Window brings trade-facing workflows into a unified digital service for businesses, regulators, and operational teams.

My work focused on making fragmented regulatory processes easier to understand, navigate, and complete across a high-stakes public service environment.

Challenge

  • Mapping dense trade and compliance procedures into clear digital flows.
  • Supporting multiple user types with different levels of regulatory familiarity.
  • Making status, requirements, and next actions legible throughout long workflows.

Solution

  • Structured core user journeys around task clarity, progress, and document readiness.
  • Designed interface patterns for forms, workflow states, review steps, and account actions.
  • Aligned product experience and visual language so the service felt consistent across touchpoints.

Outcome

  • Created a clearer foundation for users moving through trade and regulatory tasks.
  • Helped reduce ambiguity around process status, required information, and next steps.
  • Established reusable patterns for a broad public-sector product surface.
